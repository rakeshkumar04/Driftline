    Track

    Throttle is always on. Steer with left and right. Hold Brake to break traction and slide through corners. Long, fast slides build a multiplier up to ×5. Hitting a tree loses the slide you were building. In a race you start last of four. Each race runs a random 3 to 10 laps. Tyres wear, faster when you slide. To pit, keep to the marked side before the start straight and the car drives itself through the stop. Races of 6 laps or more need at least one stop, or you get 15 seconds added. Tuck in behind a rival on a straight to catch their slipstream.

    Keyboard: ← → or A D to steer, Space or ↓ to brake, P to pause.
